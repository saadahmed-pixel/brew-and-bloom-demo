Why Brew & Bloom

Chosen for one thing: a better cup of coffee.

We obsess over the details other roasters skip — who grows the beans, when they're roasted, and how they reach your kitchen. Here's what that means for you.

🌱

Ethically sourced

We work directly with family farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Peru. Paying 30% above fair-trade prices means growers can invest in quality — and the quality shows up in every cup.

⏱️

Fresh to order

Coffee is a fresh food. We never roast ahead of demand — your bag is roasted the day you order it and shipped within 48 hours, so it peaks right as it lands on your counter.

🏆

Guaranteed great

If a bag isn't your cup, we'll replace it or refund you — no returns, no fuss, no questions asked. Love the roast or it's free.

🗺️

Single-origin transparency

Every bag is traceable to its region, farm, and harvest. Scan the pack and meet the people who grew your beans.

📦

Subscriptions that adapt

Pause, skip, or swap origins any month. Your subscription follows your taste — it's coffee school at home.

🤝

Community-trained roasters

We run free cuppings and brewing workshops every month. When you buy from us, you're joining a community, not just a checkout.
