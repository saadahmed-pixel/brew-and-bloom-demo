Our story

It started with one imperfect cup.

From a garage roaster to a small roastery with a big idea: coffee can taste better, and the people who grow it can be paid better too.

Sweating over beans in 2018

Brew & Bloom began in a one-car garage in 2018, with a second-hand roaster, a notebook full of failed batches, and a stubborn belief that there had to be a better way. The first bag we ever sold went to a neighbor — and she politely asked for her money back.

Instead of quitting, we got curious. We visited farms in Ethiopia and Colombia, sat with growers, cupped hundreds of coffees, and learned that the best cups always came from people who were paid fairly for their work. That insight shaped everything: we'd buy directly from farmers, roast in small batches, and ship only after you order.

Today we roast every week in a sunlit roastery, still by hand, still small-batch. The neighbors who laughed at our first roast are now some of our most loyal subscribers — which feels like the best kind of redemption.
